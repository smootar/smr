The Shingle We Trust

by IKO

IKO Dynasty with ArmourZone

Our standard architectural shingle. It carries the ratings that matter in Utah — wind, hail and fire — and it is backed by a limited lifetime manufacturer warranty on top of our own workmanship coverage.

  • 130 MPH limited wind warrantyArmourZone — a 1¼" reinforced woven nailing band — gives the fasteners far more to hold onto than a standard strip.
  • Class 3 impact resistanceMay qualify you for a homeowner insurance discount — worth a call to your agent to confirm before you choose.
  • FastLock sealantBonds tighter as it warms in the sun, which is exactly what you want going into a Utah summer after a spring install.
  • Algae-resistant granulesBuilt in, not sprayed on — they resist the blue-green streaking that ages a roof visually long before it fails.
  • Class A fire ratingASTM E108 / UL 790 — the highest rating available for a residential roof covering.

Limited lifetime warranty includes a 15-year Iron Clad Protection period, a 130 MPH limited wind warranty and a 10-year limited algae-resistance warranty. See IKO's limited warranty for complete terms, conditions and application requirements.

Every Roof We Install

A complete system, not just shingles

A roof is only as good as what sits under and around it. This is standard on every replacement we quote — never an upsell.

Full tear-off

Old material removed down to the deck and hauled away — no layering over a failing roof.

Deck inspection

Every sheet checked for rot and soft spots once it is exposed, with findings shown to you.

Synthetic underlayment

Tear-resistant and far more stable than felt across Utah's temperature swings.

Ice & water shield

Self-sealing membrane in the valleys and eaves where ice dams do their damage.

New flashing

Chimneys, walls, valleys and penetrations reflashed — reused flashing is where leaks start.

Ridge ventilation

Balanced intake and exhaust so the attic breathes — it protects the deck and the shingles above it.

Drip edge & starter

New metal edging plus a proper starter course and matching ridge cap on every plane.

Magnetic nail sweep

Yard, driveway and walkways swept at the end of every day — not just the last one.
